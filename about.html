About EasyForm Tools

Welcome to EasyForm Tools, a simple website created to make everyday photo and document tasks easier.

What We Offer

Our website currently provides tools to resize and compress photos and convert images into PDF files. These tools can be useful when preparing images for online forms, applications, and everyday document needs.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make common digital tasks simple and accessible through a straightforward website that works on mobile phones and computers.

Privacy and Simplicity

Our photo and image-to-PDF tools are designed to process selected images in your browser rather than upload them to our own server. Please read our Privacy Policy for more information.
